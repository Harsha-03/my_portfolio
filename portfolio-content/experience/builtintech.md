# BuiltinTech: Co-Founder, Product Designer & Frontend Engineer

## Overview

BuiltinTech was a consumer-facing web product studio Harsha co-founded in
January 2023 and ran through December 2023. Over 12 months, he shipped 10+
paid client products across four verticals (construction, minerals, fitness,
infrastructure) as the sole designer on every project.

The scale came from a repeatable intake-to-ship pattern, not one-off custom
work. Same designer, discovery through post-launch. No handoff friction
between design and engineering because Harsha owned both.

---

## Timeline

- **Period:** January 2023 – December 2023
- **Location:** India
- **Role:** Co-Founder, Product Designer & Frontend Engineer
- **Verticals:** Construction, Minerals, Fitness, Infrastructure

---

## The Velocity Pattern

3–4 week ship cycles were the norm, not the exception. A six-step pattern
replaced custom scoping on every project:

1. **Intake**: client brief, requirement discovery, scoping
2. **IA**: service-first hierarchy applied across every vertical
3. **Design**: Figma flows, page structures, component patterns
4. **Build**: HTML/CSS/JS/Flask implementation
5. **Deploy**: Vercel deployment, DNS, SSL
6. **Handoff**: client walkthrough, editable content, ongoing support

Reusable component patterns meant the second and third projects in the same
vertical shipped faster than the first. Featured client Dharani Minerals
shipped end-to-end in 2023.

---

## Design Practice

- Translated ambiguous client briefs into user flows, page hierarchies, and
  component-driven interfaces that could actually be built and shipped
- Designed responsive layouts as the default, not an afterthought
- Built and maintained component libraries so second and third projects in
  the same vertical inherited working patterns from the first
- Balanced UX clarity, client expectations, technical constraints, and
  delivery timelines on every project

---

## What This Experience Built

- **Velocity**: the ability to ship a real consumer web product in 3–4 weeks
- **Volume tolerance**: 10+ concurrent client relationships without dropping
  design quality
- **End-to-end ownership**: comfortable owning discovery, design, build,
  deploy, and handoff without a team safety net
- **Systems thinking**: the intake-to-ship pattern is a systems-level
  design decision, not a workflow accident
