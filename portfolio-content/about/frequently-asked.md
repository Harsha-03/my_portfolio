## Design Process

Harsha's design process depends on the problem class, but a recurring pattern
across his shipped work:

1. **Diagnose the actual problem.** The ticket is a symptom. Sit with the
   product, identify what's actually broken.
2. **Reject the obvious fix if it's the wrong one.** Every case study includes
   at least one rejected alternative. GPS pickup, queue counter, and barista
   cam were all rejected before landing on the four-state model for the
   Missing State case study. Full-row status washes were rejected before
   landing on the 3px left border pattern at Phoenix AI.
3. **Prototype the reframe.** Figma flows, low-fi first, then interactive.
4. **Ship it.** Ship authority is earned by defending decisions with clear
   reasoning, not by asking for approval.
5. **Document the decision.** Every reframe gets written down: as a case
   study, a Medium post, or a Slack message to the team.
6. **Iterate on real usage.** No design decision is final. Post-launch
   iteration is where the real learning happens.
